# ARCUS Expert Evidence Contribution Policy

Status: implemented intake workflow; editorial review required

## Purpose

ARCUS accepts documented contributions from researchers, practitioners,
institutions and qualified witnesses to improve the completeness and
traceability of the Italian bridge-collapse archive. Contributions may concern
an existing record, a missing event, a source, technical context or media.

Submission is not publication and is not scientific validation. No public
record is modified automatically.

The public intake combines a hidden-field spam trap with a bounded submission
rate. Production deployment should additionally place the endpoint behind the
platform-wide edge protection and retention policy.

## Editorial workflow

1. ARCUS assigns a unique contribution identifier and records the submission.
2. The editorial centre verifies event identity, source provenance,
   internal consistency and image rights.
3. A submission may remain under review, require clarification, be accepted,
   rejected or archived. A rejection does not imply that the contributor acted
   in bad faith; it may reflect insufficient or non-verifiable evidence.
4. Accepted evidence is incorporated only through the normal curated master
   workflow and appears in a future versioned release and changelog.
5. Contributor credit follows the stated preference and the licence or
   permission attached to the material.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer primary technical reports, institutional documents, peer-reviewed
  publications and stable archives.
- Separate observed facts from technical interpretation and legal attribution.
- Direct professional knowledge and eyewitness accounts are admissible as
  leads, but require corroboration before changing a factual record.
- Documents that are not available online are admissible. The contributor must
  provide a title, author or issuing body, document type and, where available,
  date, relevant pages and archive reference. A private PDF is optional: a
  contributor may instead offer inspection on request or only a verifiable
  archival reference.
- Do not submit sensitive personal data, confidential documents without
  permission, or unsupported allegations about people or organisations.
- Conflicting sources remain visible to the editor; uncertainty is recorded
  rather than resolved by silent inference.

## Images

An image requires creator, credit line and a declared rights basis. Accepted
bases are own work with permission to ARCUS, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain or
documented institutional permission. A public URL alone is not evidence of a
licence. Submitted files are stored privately until editorial acceptance.

Private documents are stored separately from the public release. Supplying a
copy authorises editorial inspection only; publication requires a separate,
explicit rights decision.

## Contributor recognition

Each submission receives a stable identifier. Accepted contributors who opt in
may be acknowledged by name, affiliation and ORCID in the relevant release.
Public recognition is based on accepted evidence, never on submission volume;
ARCUS does not use rankings, badges or popularity metrics.

## Governance boundary

The contributor supplies evidence; the ARCUS editor remains responsible for
curation, taxonomy alignment, versioning and publication. Accepted
contributions must never be presented as a popularity vote or as proof of
collapse causation beyond the supporting sources.
