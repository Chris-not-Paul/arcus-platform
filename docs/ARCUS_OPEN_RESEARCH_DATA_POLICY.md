# ARCUS Open Research Data Policy

ARCUS Open Research exposes a curated, revisable evidence base without a mandatory account. The prepared release is `arcus-open-2026.15`, covers 2000-2026, and contains 260 events, 1057 source records, 9 source-supported shared episodes (72 events) and 105 taxonomy entries. Passing automated QA does not certify every historical claim: documentary limitations remain explicit.

## Public data

Open includes the canonical `ITxx.xx.xx` scientific event identifier, stable slug, date, coordinates, municipality/province/region, bridge and infrastructure attributes, severity, casualties, general and specific cause, trigger, observed process, involved component, evidence level, source confidence, location quality, publishable curation level, description, shared-episode membership, taxonomy, dictionary, methodology metadata, limitations, citation, changelog, audit, aggregate statistics, CSV and GeoJSON. Legacy `B` identifiers are confined to the versioned migration mapping.

`structural_type` records only the documented load-bearing system. Terms such as viaduct and overpass remain in names or crossing context and are not treated as structural systems. When the structural system is not supported by the available evidence, the value remains unavailable rather than being inferred from the asset name.

Source links are published only when valid HTTP/HTTPS URLs. Text references are retained in `source_reference` and are never rendered as links. The release does not redistribute third-party articles or reports.

The D'Angelo, Ballio and Ravazzani (2025) article is retained for all 210 Hydraulic events, distinguishing scientific context from reviewed dataset provenance. The additional Lenzino citation is retained after its cause reclassification. Its 2000-2023 study scope is not event-specific evidence for every linked record, especially later events. Author-dataset length/process values may be accepted where crossing identity is matched, even if news sources omit those fields.

`collapse_scope` separates bridge structures, multiple components, approach embankments, temporary works and unspecified scope. Approach collapses remain in the overall total; the operational extension is not literal NYSDOT span-collapse compliance. Unknown casualty counts are not rendered as zero. Italian summaries preserve access to original documentary notes.

Earlier ARCUS releases are superseded for current consultation. They remain recoverable in the private archive; no external DOI dataset is modified by this release.

## Access and license

Base consultation and downloads require no account. Free accounts may support saved views, collections, watchlists, preferences and release notifications only. ARCUS-authored metadata is declared CC BY-NC 4.0 for non-commercial reuse; commercial use requires separate permission. Linked third-party content retains its original rights and terms.

## Exclusions

Open excludes credentials, provider caches, sessions, account data, customer uploads and inventories, customer rankings/reports, sensitive editorial notes, restricted source content, non-publishable AINOP denominators, unvalidated mitigation rules and operational backend configuration.

## Scientific limits

The dataset records documented historical events. Counts and percentages are observed database frequencies, not failure probabilities, causal estimates, inspection results or engineering prescriptions.
