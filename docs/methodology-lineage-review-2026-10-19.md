# Scientific lineage and methodology wording — 19 October 2026

## Scope

Editorial clarification only. No changes to event records, sources, Master, classification rules, licences or release artefacts. The public dataset remains `arcus-open-2026.17`.

## Changes

- Add a bilingual methodology section, `#dataset-lineage`, describing the original collective dataset, review of existing fields, subsequent additions and curator responsibility.
- Distinguish retaining a value, revising a record and independently revalidating every detail. Do not extend the original article's peer-review status to subsequent ARCUS releases.
- Retain attribution to original coauthors and distinguish reused S3 lengths/classifications from new ARCUS measurements or analyses. Scientific-context citations are not automatic event-specific evidence.
- Avoid unconditional claims that every event identity is resolved or that every listed source corroborates every field.
- Clarify source-record counts versus unique documents and independent corroboration.
- Describe date-range information as retained in documentary notes, matching the current public-copy structure.
- Link Publications to the methodology explanation and use shared text for visible pages and crawlable static HTML.
- Keep institutional interest distinct from endorsement: no ANSFISA approval, validation or partnership is claimed.

## Editorial basis and limits

The comparison with the original file frozen in the public Mendeley repository confirmed revisions to existing fields, not just additional hydraulic data. This supports qualitative explanation of subsequent curation. Comparison counts are deliberately not embedded as live methodology statistics: they refer to a specific baseline, mapping and release and do not establish full independent validation.
