# ARCUS brand mark

## Positioning

The ARCUS mark combines the initial **A** with a structural bridge form. The identity is intended to read as a scientific observatory rather than a generic technology product.

Primary descriptor: **Bridge Failure Observatory**.

The broader typography, colour and component rules are defined in `ARCUS_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md`.

## Asset system

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `logo-mark.svg` | Compact mark, map controls and small institutional applications |
| `logo-horizontal-dark.svg` | Primary wordmark on light surfaces |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` | Reversed wordmark on dark surfaces |
| `logo-full-light.svg` | Full institutional lockup on light surfaces |
| `logo-full.svg` | Full institutional lockup on dark surfaces |
| `public/favicon.svg` | Transparent browser and installed-app mark |

The ARCUS wordmark is based on **IBM Plex Sans Medium**, published by IBM under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The lettering is converted to vector paths rather than live text. This keeps it stable across browsers, exported figures and systems without the font installed.

## Core colours

- Observatory teal: `#173F42`
- Structural copper: `#A76532`
- Signal copper on dark surfaces: `#C58A32`
- Scientific ink: `#202826`
- Paper: `#F3F0E8`
- Paper white: `#FCFBF7`

## Usage rules

- Preserve the SVG aspect ratio and clear space around the mark.
- Use the light-surface or dark-surface asset as supplied; do not recolour individual elements ad hoc.
- Do not place the detailed full lockup where the descriptor becomes unreadable; use the horizontal wordmark or mark instead.
- Do not add gradients, bevels, outlines or AI-style glow effects to the logo.
- Keep **Bridge Failure Observatory** as an institutional descriptor, not as a claim of prediction, certification or safety assessment.
